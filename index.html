Welcome to SmartPantry

SmartPantry helps you keep track of what's in your kitchen, decide what to use, and get smart suggestions on what to cook or buy next.

Here's how to use this site:

  • Add – enter an item's name and quantity to add it to your pantry list. You can delete individual items or clear the whole list at once.
  • Select – tick the checkboxes next to items you want to use, then hit Confirm to send them onward to AI. Deselect All clears every checkbox in one click.
  • AI – based on whatever you selected, this page will suggest recipes, substitutes, or what to restock.
  • Logout – securely sign out and return to the login page.

Tip: keep your pantry list updated after every grocery run so AI suggestions stay accurate.
